Reflow, Resize and Spacing

People enlarge text, zoom the page, narrow the viewport, or inject extra letter and line spacing. Layouts that rely on fixed boxes, clipping, or stacked UI often lose content or controls. Use the playground controls, then try browser zoom and a narrow window too.

Fixed length and width

Pixel widths, non-wrapping toolbars, and rigid columns fight reflow (about 320 CSS px) and text resize. At the default size these may look fine — enlarge the font or narrow the window to see them break.

Overlapping banners and z-index

Sticky or absolutely positioned chrome with a high z-index can cover interactive controls when the layout reflows on a small screen or larger text.
